Does TruckTap work without internet?

Yes. The clicker, pick-list, templates, and local data work offline. Team Sync is optional and sends queued changes when connected.

Is there a required subscription?

No. Buy once and keep using the app. Updates are included for your plan term; renewal is optional. Team Sync is the only recurring product.

Can we use phones and desktop?

Yes. Run the Windows desktop app, the installable PWA, or both. Team Sync keeps devices on the same workspace when enabled.

How do we activate a license?

After checkout, copy the signed key from the license page and paste it into License & Downloads in the app.

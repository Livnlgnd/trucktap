About TruckTap

Built for the people who keep things running.

TruckTap exists because the end-of-shift restock list is still too often a clipboard, a group text, and a memory. Field teams deserve a tool that works as hard as they do.

Local-first by design

A van’s stock list is operational data. It should not stop working because a basement has no signal. TruckTap saves every tap to the device first. Team Sync is an optional layer for teams that need devices to see the same board, not a requirement for using the app.

Simple under pressure

Large controls, obvious labels, and keyboards that never block the next tap. The workflow should survive gloves, rain, and a bad day.

Honest pricing

Buy once. Own it. No mandatory subscription for the core app. Optional Team Sync exists for teams that need live shared data.

Built for both sides

The field needs speed. The shop needs an order it can actually pick. TruckTap keeps both sides looking at the same list.

Who we build for

Electrical, plumbing, HVAC, multifamily, and maintenance crews.

TruckTap is a product of Vybelive LLC, a small software company focused on practical, local-first tools for property and field operations. We build on the principle that accountability comes from a simple, honest process — not from more paperwork.

FAQ

Quick fixes.

Where do I enter my license key?

Open the app and select License & Downloads in the top navigation. Paste the key into the activation field and click Activate License. The key is verified offline.

My checkout finished, but I don’t have the key.

Open the license lookup page and paste the Stripe session ID from your receipt. The session ID starts with cs_live_ or cs_test_.

Does the app work without Team Sync?

Yes. The clicker, templates, pick-list, and local CSV export work without any server. Team Sync is only for keeping multiple devices on the same shared board.

Can I move TruckTap to another computer?

Windows installations store data on that computer. Back up your local data before moving devices. A license can be removed from one device and activated on another within the plan’s device limits.

What happens after my update window?

Your purchased version keeps working. Optional maintenance renewals just extend access to newer builds and support.

How do I request a refund?

Contact support with the checkout session ID from your Stripe receipt. Refund eligibility is governed by the Terms of Service.

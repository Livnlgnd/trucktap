Privacy policy

Your field data is yours.

TruckTap is local-first. We aim to collect as little as possible and make the hosted parts of the product explicit.

Effective date: November 21, 2026

Local app data

In local mode, your vans, parts, tap counts, restock orders, templates, and license key are stored in IndexedDB and localStorage on your device. This local data is not sent to TruckTap unless you explicitly use Team Sync.

Team Sync

If you create or join a Team Sync workspace, the shared van board, parts, orders, account name, email address, workspace membership, and order history are stored in our hosted Postgres database. The hosted API is used to synchronize those records between your devices. You can disconnect Team Sync in the app; disconnecting stops future sync but does not automatically delete workspace data.

Payments

License purchases are processed by Stripe. TruckTap does not receive or store payment card numbers. Stripe receives the billing details and payment information you provide during checkout. We receive confirmation of payment, a checkout session identifier, customer name/email, and the purchased plan so we can issue a signed license key.

License activation

Signed license keys are verified on your device. We do not require an account or a periodic online check to use a local license. License retrieval uses the Stripe checkout session ID and does not require a TruckTap account.

Analytics, cookies, and ads

TruckTap does not include third-party advertising, cross-site tracking pixels, or product analytics. We use browser storage for your theme preference, active view, selected van, trial state, and license. Third-party services such as Stripe and Google Fonts may process data under their own privacy policies.

Data retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you clear it, uninstall the app, or clear browser storage. Team Sync workspace data remains until you disconnect and request deletion. To request deletion of hosted workspace data or purchase records, contact support through the public issue tracker and include enough information to locate the workspace or checkout session. Never post a full license key in a public issue.

Children

TruckTap is a business tool and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy as the product evolves. The effective date above will change when we do.

Contact

TruckTap is operated by Vybelive LLC. For privacy questions, use TruckTap support.

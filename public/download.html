Already have a license?

Activate in under a minute.

Buy once, activate with your signed license key, and keep working on the same local board. You can move the key between devices when you need to.

Retrieve my license See pricing
  1. Install the app. Use the Windows installer or add the PWA to your home screen.
  2. Open License & Downloads. It is the key-shaped tab in the app.
  3. Paste your key. TruckTap verifies it locally, and your plan unlocks immediately.

iPhone & iPad

  1. Open app.html in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.

Android

  1. Open app.html in Chrome.
  2. Open the ⋮ menu.
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen.

Desktop browsers

  1. Open app.html in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Click the install icon in the address bar.
  3. Confirm the install.
